Argument glossary — role codes and definitions

Codes describe argument roles. A record's type and references establish its role. The model extends Toulmin's claim, grounds, warrant, backing, qualifier and rebuttal with engineering records.

C — Claim

States the proposition the argument seeks to justify within its scope.

What should the reader accept?

S — Subclaim

States an intermediate conclusion whose support contributes to another claim.

Which intermediate proposition needs its own argument?

G — Ground

States a premise used by an inference and records its evidence and support status.

What premise supports this conclusion, and what supports that premise?

E — Evidence

Records supplied source material used to assess a premise, assumption, warrant or challenge.

Which retained source can a reviewer inspect?

W — Warrant

States the rule or reasoning that connects specified premises to a conclusion under stated conditions.

Why do these premises justify this conclusion?

B — Backing

Provides support for the rule or reasoning used by a warrant.

Why should the reader accept this warrant in this setting?

Q — Qualifier

States the justified force or uncertainty of a claim.

How strongly does the available support justify the claim?

R — Rebuttal conditions

Identifies an exception or boundary condition that would defeat or restrict the inference.

When would this inference cease to justify its conclusion?

A — Assumption

States a condition relied upon by specified parts of the argument, with its support status and proposed validation.

What condition must hold for this reasoning to apply?

D — Defeater

Records a challenge to a premise, inference or conclusion, together with its basis, response and disposition.

What could undermine this premise, undercut this inference or rebut this conclusion?

N — Evidence need

Specifies an investigation that could resolve missing support or a challenge and identifies the claim it would strengthen.

What should be checked, how will its result be judged, and which claim could it strengthen?

ARG — Argument record

Presents one warrant with its premises, conclusion, support and applicable conditions.

Which complete inference route is being assessed?

current_claim — Current claim

States the present conclusion being assessed, with its recorded qualification and assessment status.

What conclusion is the argument currently asking the reader to accept?

stronger_target — Stronger target

States a stronger candidate claim whose support depends on further evidence or reasoning.

What stronger conclusion could further investigation justify?

scope — Scope

Defines the system boundary and the configurations, operating conditions and period covered by the argument.

Exactly which cases does the claim cover?

criterion — Assessment criterion

States the condition used to judge a proposed check's result, with a justification appropriate to the claim.

What result would count as adequate support?

composition — Support composition

Records how inference routes combine to support a conclusion and which routes the assessment relies upon.

Must these routes hold together, contribute cumulatively or provide alternatives?

alternative — Competing explanation or option

States another explanation or option and a check that could distinguish it from the proposed account.

What other account fits the observations, and how could it be distinguished?

assessment — Current assessment

States the support status of the current claim and explains the decisive reasoning and remaining limitations.

What does the assessed argument justify now, and why?

revision_trigger — Revision trigger

Identifies a change or investigation result that requires reassessing a claim or inference.

What event should cause the assessment to be revisited?

residual_risk — Residual risk

States a consequential uncertainty or failure possibility that remains after the recorded assessment and controls.

What consequential possibility remains unresolved?

decision_limit — Decision limit

States a restriction on the actions or decisions justified by the current argument.

Which decisions remain outside the support established here?

decision_basis — Decision basis

Explains why a proposed action meets the stated objective and acceptance criterion, considering feasible alternatives and the consequences of error.

Why is this action justified under the stated constraints?
